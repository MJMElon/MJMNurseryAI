🎛️ Portal View & Function
Function 📱 555 FC Portal 👷 555 Worker Portal
Switching off is always obeyed. A few switches have nothing behind their ON yet, and their row says which. Scan, Delivery Order, PALMS and Culling Calculator are not built for a worker's phone — leaving them on shows a worker nothing today, and switches them on the day one is. Upload photo is stored and not yet acted on: a worker signs in with a PIN, which the storage bucket does not take uploads from, and the key their phone holds is public, so opening the bucket to it would open it to anyone.
🗺️ Boundary
A KML or GPX outline for each nursery, uploaded one at a time. It is drawn behind the path on the GPS track map, so somebody walking a plot can see where their nursery ends — a worker sees the nurseries inside their own boundary, a Field Conductor sees them all. After a device has opened that map once with signal, the outlines stay on it offline.
Reading…
Drawn on the GPS track map. Anybody recording a walked track — a worker in the 555 Worker Portal, a Field Conductor in the FC Portal — sees the outline behind their path, in yellow. It is a line and not a shaded area, so it never hides the beds underneath it, and it cannot be tapped by mistake while walking.

A phone keeps the last outlines it read, so once a device has opened the map with signal it draws them in a plot with none. Removing one here takes it off those phones the next time each has signal. A nursery with no file uploaded simply draws no line, which is not an error.
