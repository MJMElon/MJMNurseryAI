What the work actually costs in days

Read off the plot log recorded in the field, not off the ideal-day table. The unit of measurement is a cycle — Saringan Anak Bibit through to Pengambilan, one intake worked from start to sale — so a plot that has gone round three times contributes three figures, and the shortest and the longest of them are what a motion study is after. A measurement counts in the month it finished.
