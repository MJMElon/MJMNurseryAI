User Setting

What pinning does

Everyone granted Nelos on the main portal's User Access appears here — there is nothing to add by hand. Pin a person to the module whose cases they handle, and from anywhere in the system they will see that module's queue plus anything assigned to them personally. So an auditor pinned to Audit Portal still gets their To-Do list on the Seedling Stock system.
Somebody not pinned yet sees every case, and a Nelos admin always does.

Where a new case goes

A case raised in a section is worked by the handlers of whichever section you point it at — an Audit Portal case going to Nursery Operation, an FC Portal case going to Audit. Leave it on itself and the section works its own cases. Untick Can raise and that section stops offering the case form at all.
This applies when a case is created; moving an existing case is done on the case itself.
